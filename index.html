Benefits Education

Understanding the HSA Plan and Traditional Plan

The HSA Plan generally has lower employee premiums and higher deductible exposure. The Traditional Plan generally has higher employee premiums and lower deductible exposure. Both plans include preventive care and routine vision benefits. Voluntary Vision provides additional vision benefits such as frame, lens, and contact allowances.

What is an HSA?

An HSA is a tax-advantaged account available to eligible employees enrolled in the HSA Plan. Contributions may be made through payroll or during the year, funds roll over, the account belongs to the employee, and eligible funds may be invested. Qualified expenses include medical, dental, vision, prescription, deductible, copayment, and coinsurance expenses.

2026 limits: $4,400 for employee-only coverage and $8,750 for family coverage, plus a $1,000 catch-up contribution for eligible individuals age 55 or older. Limits include employee and employer contributions.

Flexible Spending Accounts

Health Care and Limited Purpose FSAs have a $3,400 annual limit for the plan year beginning July 1, 2026. Up to $680 may roll over. The Dependent Care FSA limit is $7,500, or $3,750 if married filing separately, and has no rollover.

Employees enrolled in the HSA Plan cannot enroll in a full Health Care FSA. A Limited Purpose FSA can only be used for eligible dental and vision expenses. FSA elections are generally made during new-hire enrollment, open enrollment, or after a qualifying event, and must be re-elected each year.

Voluntary Disability Insurance

Short-Term Disability may replace 60% of weekly pre-disability earnings, subject to plan limits and waiting periods. Long-Term Disability is intended for qualifying disabilities lasting beyond the LTD waiting period. Eligibility depends on VRS plan and service time.

Voluntary Critical Illness

Critical Illness coverage pays a lump-sum benefit upon diagnosis of a covered condition. Employee and spouse coverage are available at $10,000 or $20,000. Spouse coverage cannot exceed employee coverage. Dependent children are automatically covered at 50% of the employee election at no additional cost.

Optional Group Life Insurance

Employees may elect one to eight times creditable compensation, subject to plan maximums. Spouse and child coverage must follow the same option row. Some elections may require Evidence of Insurability. Basic group life is County-provided and is separate from optional life.

Educational Information

The calculator presents factual information to help employees understand available options. It does not recommend a plan, contribution amount, or benefit election. Employees should review official plan documents and consult personal advisors as appropriate.
